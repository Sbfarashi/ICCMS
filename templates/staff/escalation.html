Escalation Policy
  • Level 1
    Complaint has exceeded the normal response time and requires immediate staff attention.
  • Level 2
    Complaint remains unresolved after assignment and should be reviewed by a supervisor.
  • Level 3
    Complaint has become critical and requires management intervention.
Best Practices
  • Assign high-priority complaints immediately.
  • Monitor complaints approaching escalation thresholds.
  • Ensure engineers update complaint progress regularly.
  • Review unresolved complaints during daily operations meetings.
  • Notify supervisors promptly for Level 2 and Level 3 escalations.
  • Keep customers informed throughout the resolution process.
